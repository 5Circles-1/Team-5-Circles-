====================================================================
  5C PULSE  —  START HERE
====================================================================

You have two things in this folder. You only need the first one today.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
1)  THE DASHBOARD  —  works right now, nothing to install
--------------------------------------------------------------------

    Double-click:      OPEN-ME-Dashboard.html

That's it. It opens in Chrome, Edge or Safari like any web page and
the dashboard is there, already filled in with your team.

    - Pick your name at the TOP RIGHT to see the day as that person.
    - Admins (Arjun, Priya) also get a "Setup" tab.
    - Click "Turn on alerts" once so the alarm can make sound.
    - Press the "Test" button, top right, to hear the alarm.

Everything saves automatically in that browser on that computer.

WHAT IT CANNOT DO:
    There is no password and no login in this file. Whoever opens it
    can pick any name. And a ping rings on THIS computer (and its
    other tabs) — it cannot reach a teammate's laptop, because there
    is no server involved. It is for trying the thing out and for
    running your own day, not yet for the whole team.

    For real logins and pings that reach people, see (2).


--------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

"Nothing happens when I double-click the HTML file."
    Right-click it, choose "Open with", and pick Chrome or Edge.

"I hear no sound."
    Click the blue "Turn on alerts" bar at the top once. Browsers
    block sound until you click something. Then press "Test".

"How do I add a team member?"
    Setup tab -> Team -> type their name, pick their department,
    press Add. They immediately get that department's daily tasks,
    schedule and training list.

"How do I give someone a job to do?"
    "Give work" tab -> tap their face -> one line -> a date -> send.

"I want to start with my real team, not the sample one."
    Setup tab -> scroll to the bottom -> "Start from an empty team".
