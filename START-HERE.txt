  5C PULSE  —  START HERE
====================================================================

Everything you need is one file:

        OPEN-ME.html

Double-click it (extract this zip first if you got it as a zip:
right-click -> Extract All -> Desktop). It opens in Chrome or Edge
like a web page, and it contains:

    1.  A LIVE DEMO of the tool, right on the page. Play with it:
        set yourself up as the admin, add a teammate, give a task,
        ring the bell. Nothing you do there is saved anywhere.

    2.  THE SETUP GUIDE — six short steps that put the real tool on
        the internet using your own Google account
        (webinars5circles@gmail.com). Takes about 10 minutes, costs
        nothing, and gives you ONE WEB ADDRESS the whole team opens
        on their phones.

Why the online version matters: everyone gets their own PIN login,
everything is saved in a Google Sheet in YOUR Drive, and the bell
rings on the OTHER person's phone — things a file on one computer
can never do.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE TOOL DOES  (once it's online)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

    - Tasks: give anyone a job in one line, with a date. They see it
      the moment they open the tool. To do -> Doing -> Done.
    - "I'm stuck" flag: rings the person who gave the task, so
      nobody stays stuck quietly.
    - Chat on record: team-wide or one-to-one. Every word is kept in
      your Google Sheet.
    - The bell: a message that takes over the screen and keeps
      ringing until the person answers - and you see their answer.
    - Team tab (admins): add people, hand out PINs, reset PINs,
      remove people. When someone leaves, their open tasks move to
      you so nothing is lost.

Logins are a face + a 4-digit PIN. You hand out the PINs; the tool
shows each one to you exactly once. Five wrong guesses locks a
login for five minutes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

"Where does our data live?"
    In a Google Sheet called "5C Pulse - Team Data", in the Drive of
    the account that did the setup. The Team tab has a button that
    opens it.

"Someone forgot their PIN."
    Any admin: Team tab -> their card -> Reset PIN. A fresh PIN is
    shown to you once.

"I want to hear the bell."
    Sounds start after your first tap on the page (that's how
    browsers work). Phones: volume up, keep the tab open during
    work hours.

"I changed my mind about a name / the company name."
    Team tab (admins) - company name is at the bottom.
