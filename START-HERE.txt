  5C PULSE  —  START HERE
====================================================================

Everything you need is one file:

        OPEN-ME.html

Double-click it (extract this zip first if you got it as a zip:
right-click -> Extract All -> Desktop). It opens in Chrome or Edge
like a web page, and it contains:

    1.  A LIVE DEMO of the tool, right on the page. Play with it:
        set yourself up as the admin, add a teammate, give a task,
        ring the bell. Nothing you do there is saved anywhere.

    2.  THE SETUP GUIDE — six short steps that put the real tool on
        the internet using your own Google account
        (webinars5circles@gmail.com). Takes about 10 minutes, costs
        nothing, and gives you ONE WEB ADDRESS the whole team opens
        on their phones.

Why the online version matters: everyone gets their own PIN login,
everything is saved in a Google Sheet in YOUR Drive, and the bell
rings on the OTHER person's phone — things a file on one computer
can never do.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE TOOL DOES  (once it's online)
--------------------------------------------------------------------

    - Tasks: give anyone a job in one line, with a date. They see it
      the moment they open the tool. To do -> Doing -> Done.
    - "I'm stuck" flag: rings the person who gave the task, so
      nobody stays stuck quietly.
    - Updates tab: post what got done, attach files or screenshots,
      and tick "Needs review" when you want someone to check it -
      they mark it "Reviewed" and everyone sees who did.
    - Chat on record: team-wide or one-to-one, now instant, with
      file attachments. Every word is kept in your Google Sheet.
    - The bell: a message that takes over the screen and keeps
      ringing until the person answers - and you see their answer.
      It now checks the tool every few seconds, so it rings within
      moments. If the person hasn't opened the tool lately, the
      ring also lands in their EMAIL automatically.
    - Send email: from anyone's profile - for teammates who aren't
      in the tool, or for sharing beyond office hours.
    - Profiles: everyone uploads their own photo and keeps their
      mobile number and email on their card. Tap any card to see a
      person's designation (e.g. Receptionist), phone (tap to call
      or WhatsApp) and email.
    - Daily duties: give a person a standing job - like the
      receptionist's "Get GMB profile reviews" - and a task for it
      appears on their list automatically every morning.
    - Light and dark mode: two buttons in the header, pick either.
    - Team tab (ADMINS ONLY - members can look, not touch): add
      people, set designations and duties, hand out PINs, reset
      PINs, remove people. When someone leaves, their open tasks
      move to you so nothing is lost.

Logins are a face + a 4-digit PIN. You hand out the PINs; the tool
shows each one to you exactly once. Five wrong guesses locks a
login for five minutes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

"Where does our data live?"
    In a Google Sheet called "5C Pulse - Team Data", in the Drive of
    the account that did the setup. The Team tab has a button that
    opens it.

"Someone forgot their PIN."
    Any admin: Team tab -> their card -> Reset PIN. A fresh PIN is
    shown to you once.

"I want to hear the bell."
    Sounds start after your first tap on the page (that's how
    browsers work). Phones: volume up, keep the tab open during
    work hours.

"I changed my mind about a name / the company name."
    Team tab (admins) - company name is at the bottom.

"How do I set up the receptionist's daily GMB job?"
    Team tab -> tap her card -> Designation: "Receptionist",
    Daily duty: "Get GMB profile reviews" -> Save. From then on a
    task for it appears on her list every morning, by itself.

"I already set the tool up before these new features."
    Paste the two new files over the old ones in the Apps Script
    editor, then Deploy -> Manage deployments -> pencil icon ->
    New version -> Deploy. Google will ask you to allow access
    once more (it now also uses Drive for attachments and Gmail
    for the email feature). Your people, tasks and messages are
    all kept.
